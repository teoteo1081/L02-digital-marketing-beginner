## PART 5 · Incomplete Sentences (14 câu)

*Directions: A word or phrase is missing in each of the sentences below. Four answer choices are given below each sentence. Select the best answer to complete the sentence.*

### Questions 126–129 refer to the following article.

> **From Views to Revenue: How Vietnamese Creators Actually Get Paid**
>
> [1] A few years ago, building an online following was mostly a hobby. Today, it is a real source of income for thousands of Vietnamese creators. But views alone do not pay the bills. Understanding exactly how each platform pays is the first step toward a sustainable business.
>
> [2] Affiliate marketing is the most common starting point. On TikTok Shop and Shopee, creators attach product links to their videos or livestreams and receive a commission when viewers buy. Creators do not need to hold inventory or arrange shipping, which makes this model attractive to beginners. The downside is that commission rates differ by product category and can change with little notice.
>
> [3] Advertising revenue is another option. Long-form creators can join the YouTube Partner Program once they meet the platform's eligibility requirements, after which they receive a share of the money from ads shown on their videos. This income tends to be more stable, but it grows slowly and depends heavily on how long people keep watching.
>
> [4] Finally, many creators eventually launch their own products, such as online courses, design templates, or consulting packages. They use free content to attract an audience, collect contacts through a Zalo Official Account or an email list, and then sell directly. Profit margins are much higher, but the creator must handle customer service and payments personally.
>
> [5] "Treat your channel like a funnel," advises Nguyen Hoang Anh, a former data analyst who now runs an online course business. "Track how many viewers click, how many buy, and what each sale is worth. The numbers will tell you which model to scale."
